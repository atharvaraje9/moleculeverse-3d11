Chemoinformatics Foundations

Core theoretical concepts and calculation algorithms.

Simplified Molecular-Input Line-Entry System (SMILES) is a linear chemical notation language representing molecular structures using short ASCII strings. Lowercase characters denote aromatic rings (e.g. 'c1ccccc1' for benzene), numbers indicate ring closures, and parentheses specify branching.

Lipinski's Rule evaluates whether a chemical compound possesses physicochemical properties compatible with human oral bioavailability:

  • Molecular Weight (MW) ≤ 500 g/mol
  • LogP (Lipophilicity) ≤ 5
  • Hydrogen Bond Donors (HBD) ≤ 5
  • Hydrogen Bond Acceptors (HBA) ≤ 10
